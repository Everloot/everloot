## Overview

This fixture stands in for a real dungeon page. It says what the place is and why a player would go there,
in plain words, and leaves every count to the data blocks the page type places below the text. A real page
would describe the dungeon's place in the world, the people who run it and the trouble they cause for the
farmers and miners around it. It would point players at the {item:2589} that drops along the way and at
the boss {npc:639} who waits at the end, and it would name the quest {quest:166} that sends players there.

itself, and [[test-draft]] is a draft, so it never becomes a link. Players reach it by road from the town

## Before you go

Go with a full group and someone who can heal. The tunnels are narrow, so pull one pack at a time, and
read [[test-draft]] before you set out.

## History

The mines were dug long before the brotherhood took them over. When the workers who built the city were
not paid, they left and took the mines with them, and the brotherhood grew from that grievance into the
gang that holds the place today. Blizzard's own words, "a short quote stays a quote", are the only kind
of text this section quotes.
