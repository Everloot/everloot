# WoW Forever profession add-ons and Everloot

**Reviewed November 27, 2026.** The strongest additions are a small account-wide cooldown view and a resumable leveling route inside WoW. The work already underway on crafting profit and web leveling covers much of the third add-on's ground. Use the existing Everloot price evaluator, shopping tools and data sources to connect those pieces.

## Profession Planner

The useful interaction is the **current step** beside the native profession window. A skill range and one recipe sit above the reagent shortages. The route and aggregate shopping list stay available for players who are preparing the whole bracket. Route freezing protects a shopping decision while the player buys or crafts, and explicit replanning picks up new stock and prices. The author also documents recipe swapping, choosing a custom target, faction-aware trainer/vendor guidance, Auctionator and TSM pricing support, and an optional Syndicator inventory integration. The current listing flags community fallback prices as rough because realms differ. [Author's description](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/profplanner)

The 1.2.9 release uses a greedy choice at each skill point and bounded refinement of consecutive recipe runs. It estimates expected crafts as `1 / p` using a color-based probability curve, with empirical fit inputs and an assumed Working Overtime multiplier capped at 100%. Its optional net optimization credits estimated vendor proceeds on leftover output. AH resale estimates do not drive that optimizer. A route can be useful without proving a global minimum when recipe unlock costs, supply, inventory and uncertainty are involved. The archive's license also covers its bundled community data. [Official ZIP, inspected without running](https://mediafilez.forgecdn.net/files/9848/161/ProfPlanner-1.2.9.zip)

The refinement can shift boundaries, swap recipes and reorder intermediate producers before their consumers. It falls back to a guide for incomplete observations. Its price trail includes local, shared and bundled vendor values, Auctionator, TSM and faction/global community fallback. The optimizer uses a high penalty when price is missing and estimates AH costs with a unit quote. A plan marked inexpensive may cost more at the required quantity. Alt stock uses dated same-faction/realm snapshots with optional Syndicator additions. The Auctionator export sends outstanding AH searches. The manual sharing screenshot describes the data payload. The archive also has peer discovery that exchanges contact character names, so the manual payload and peer-discovery transport are distinct. Source areas are `Optimizer.lua`, `ProfPlanner.lua`, `Alts.lua`, `Shopping.lua` and `Sync.lua`. [Official ZIP](https://mediafilez.forgecdn.net/files/9848/161/ProfPlanner-1.2.9.zip)

## Profession Cooldown Tracker Forever

The feature is a roster of timers for known spells and owned items. The defaults are a shared alchemy transmute group, Mooncloth and Salt Shaker. Ready rows can surface first. The current package adds controls for display, hiding, sorting, disabled entries, rescanning and character resets. [Author's description](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/profession-cooldown-tracker-forever)

In 1.31, the scanner leaves combat reads pending, debounces cooldown, bag and spellbook changes, and waits briefly after a successful player cast. It converts client-relative remaining time to a server-epoch expiry. It groups known shared spells into one entry and takes the longest valid remaining time. An unreadable known group member prevents the group from overwriting its previous row. An unlearned spell can remove the row after spellbook readiness. Item possession is sampled separately. The package handles empty realm names and duplicate Forever first names with GUID-backed identity and migration. [Official 1.31 ZIP](https://mediafilez.forgecdn.net/files/9820/494/ProfessionCooldownTrackerForever-1.31.zip)

Two implementation details need particular care in Everloot. The reference normalizes an out-of-range long duration into ready and can keep an old item entry when the item is no longer present. Preserve the last reliable timestamp and show an unknown or last-seen state as appropriate. Saved expiry can elapse offline, but the actual ability and inventory should be checked again when the character returns. Everloot can attach rows to its existing local character key and realm fallback, and keep a separate `observedAt` so a later valid read can correct an older expiry. ## Crafter's Journal

The strongest idea is that each view answers a distinct player decision. The route explains which ingredients and unlocks cost money, the craft batch says what is worth attempting under the latest inputs, the calculator explains the margin, the recipe journal explains where to learn it, and inventory says which surplus has value. Its listing also documents optional AH/vendor helper windows, manual price overrides, waypoints, route options and English/Spanish support. [Author's description](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/crafters-journal)

The 0.72.0 planner uses dynamic programming over skill and the previous recipe to generate candidates, then replays and refines candidates with inventory, supply depth, tool/learning costs and intermediate crafts. Coverage and feasibility affect the result before the final cost ranking. This is bounded search, so the outcome depends on the candidate set and the price and availability inputs. The current source defaults the Working Overtime multiplier to zero pending empirical evidence. The gallery advertises a bonus from an earlier state, so the effect should be checked against the current Forever client before Everloot uses it. [Planner for the 0.72.0 source tag](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Planner.lua)

- **Price and stock inputs.** Its route scanner uses Auctionator's full-scan results to build per-item price tiers. Displayed cost can extrapolate the scan median beyond the observed quantity. Owned input comes from carried bags and the reagent bag. This release disables bank stock in its reader, and route inputs are character-scoped. Its ledger can temporarily recognize recent purchases while they are en route to bags. [Scanner](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Scanner.lua) · [Core](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Core.lua) · [Ledger](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Ledger.lua)
- **Profit and quantity.** Market signals use scan history, visible supply and optional TSM regional data to limit suggested quantities. Accepted batch ideas consume shared supply before the next idea is repriced. Vendor-only crafts are checked against marginal input cost. The calculator displays expected profit on sale separately from deposit exposure. Some ranking estimates charge a deposit conservatively. Everloot can show the assumptions behind each result with its own scoped data. [Market signals](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Market.lua) · [Craft batch](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Earn.lua) · [Calculator](https://github.com/brunobasas/CraftersJournal/blob/b752f75de6f1abadc1d164c1b279d9ee4ad9966f/Calculator.lua)

## Everloot baseline and overlap

The website offers 0.3.31 and labels live-client verification pending. The main source inspected was `5c1e8cd88`. Its profession capture reads player-opened recipe and trainer windows, records colors and acquisition evidence, and retains output metadata locally. Its crafting view already covers trainer/rank information, priced reagents, output, estimated house-cut-adjusted profit, and adding reagents to the AH shopping list. A list can show recipes recorded by an alt under the same profession name. The native window has a selected-recipe estimate. [Everloot download](https://everloot.app/addon) · [Recipe and trainer capture](../addon/modules/professions.lua) · [Professions view](../addon/modules/view_professions.lua) · [Native crafting](../addon/modules/native_crafting.lua)

The public site has 12 profession pages and reports 2,494 recipes in build 1.60.1.71139 when reviewed. The baseline web planner combines game-file catalogue information with agreed player observations. The KB's conservative route prefers observed orange ranges when deriving steps from color observations. Local inventory capture for bags, opened bank and opened mail already has explicit consent and preserves the last complete dated read. It does not establish that those materials are usable by the current character. The existing AH sell check already compares vendor value, house cut and deposit risk. [Public profession hub](https://everloot.app/tools/professions) · [KB route logic](../kb/src/profession-path.ts) · [Inventory capture](../addon/modules/inventory.lua) · [AH sell check](../addon/modules/sellcheck.lua)

Two local efforts affect the recommendation:

- The web profession guide branch at `5c0cf0f10` has leveling tracks, alternative recipes, skill and source labels, modeled probability, scope-specific prices, training information and materials export/import. The route solver works backward over a skill graph and minimizes gaps, unpriced progress and expected cost in that order. It is a foundation for path generation. A materials export alone lacks the steps, prerequisites and skill windows needed to resume a route inside WoW. Live price selection and import acceptance were still open in the project handoff.
- The approved native crafting profit effort already covers consistent quotes, recipe capability per character, quantity-aware intermediate chains, vendor/AH valuation, character shopping plans, trainer guidance, depth and web parity. Its first shared evaluator is committed on its branch and native comparison was still undergoing implementation and validation. Treat these features as in progress and feed the comparison findings into that existing effort.

### Rules that affect route correctness

- **Skill gain.** Separate observed color, verified thresholds and modeled chance. A 50% chance implies two crafts per point on average. Two crafts still have a 25% chance of producing zero points under that fixed independent model. Label expected materials and let real progress update remaining amounts.
- **Unlocks.** Treat training fees, skill caps, character level, tools, reputation, currency, faction, quest hand-ins and recipe purchases as constraints. Account for one-time purchases once. Keep a gap visible when data is missing, and distinguish a known trainer from a verified source for this recipe.
- **Inventory.** Track both total requirement and what can be accessed right now. Current bags inform the immediate step. Bank, mail and alt stock can be retrieval suggestions when available and compatible. Require evidence for cross-realm transfer, and avoid counting the same stack twice across steps or plans. Owned goods reduce cash outlay and retain opportunity cost for profit estimates.
- **Quantities and prices.** Use item and recipe IDs. Consume leftovers from whole intermediate crafts before buying or making more. Show house, quote age and source. Display an unpriced or insufficient-depth result when needed. Calculate vendor proceeds, expected AH proceeds, clearing costs and deposit exposure as separate quantities. A successfully sold auction and an expired listing have different deposit treatment.
- **Player control and privacy.** The next-step view can select a known recipe, open the existing shopping search or show a guide. Purchases, crafts, posts and training stay with the player. Keep character capability, timers, inventory and reservations local. Send anonymous recipe or trainer observations through the existing Everloot path when enabled. Build from Everloot's catalogues, observations and approved price sources using its own implementation and interface. [Everloot data policy](../docs/DATA_POLICY.md) · [Source ledger](../docs/sources/LEDGER.md)

The key end-to-end check is whether a player can open a profession after logging in, see the next valid craft or unlock, know exactly what to buy, and resume after switching characters or reloading. Prices, live availability, character observations and modeled projections need to be visible at the point of decision.
