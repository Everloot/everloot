# Everloot research index

This directory is the canonical home for dated Everloot research produced by approved research passes.

## Working rule

- Save new research here as `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
- Keep research, competitor observations, and recommendations separate from implementation specs.
- Research may inform Everloot design, but it does not authorize importing third-party data, guides, assets, or undocumented APIs.
- Before implementation, promote an approved decision into the appropriate `docs/design/`, `docs/DATA_POLICY.md`, `docs/sources/LEDGER.md`, or project backlog.
- The source ledger and data policy remain authoritative for licensing and publication boundaries.
