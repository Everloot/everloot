## Who it is for

A Mage from level 10, when the first talent point arrives, to level 60. The [talent calculator](/tools/talents/mage)
reads one point a level from the game files, 51 in all, and this plan spends every one of them.
[Blizzard's Mage deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) also starts talents at level 10.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: players who level mostly on their own, by questing, and want a plan that keeps enemies slowed and at a
  distance.

## Why this plan

[Blizzard's deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) adds Frostfire Bolt, a spell learned
at level 40 that counts as both Fire and Frost, and says it is meant for builds that take both Fire and Frost talents.
In the game files, three Fire talents also reach Frost spells: Improved Fireball shortens the cast of Frostfire Bolt
as well as Fireball, Incineration raises the critical strike chance of Ice Lance among other spells, and Master of
Elements returns mana on Fire and Frost critical strikes. The calculator shows each talent's own text.

The same deep dive cut the duration of Cone of Cold, so that farmers and boosters cannot kite as many enemies, and
weakened the slow of Improved Blizzard, to rein in the area damage of Frost Mages.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: levels 10 to 40 in Frost, starting with the talents that speed up Frostbolt (Improved Frostbolt) and give
  chill effects a chance to freeze (Frostbite), then Ice Lance, Shatter and Fingers of Frost, which all work with
  frozen targets.
- Proposed: Ice Block and Cold Snap on the way, so that the plan reaches Ice Barrier at level 40.
- Proposed: levels 41 to 58 in Fire: Improved Fireball, Incineration, Ignite and Master of Elements for Frostfire
  Bolt and Ice Lance, with Improved Fire Blast to open the fourth row. The last two points go to Improved Frost Nova.
- Proposed: no points in Improved Blizzard or Improved Cone of Cold, and leveling on one or two enemies at a time
  rather than on large pulls.

## How to play it

The plan lists every point in the order it is spent, one talent a level, and opens in the calculator as this page
stores it.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: lead with Frostbolt. When a target is frozen, by Frostbite or Frost Nova, follow with Ice Lance: it deals
  more damage to frozen targets, and Shatter raises the critical strike chance of every spell against them.
- Proposed: when Fingers of Frost triggers, spend it on Ice Lance, since it lets the next spells treat the target as
  frozen.
- Proposed: keep Ice Block and Cold Snap for a fight that goes wrong. Cold Snap finishes the cooldowns of your other
  Frost spells.
- Proposed: from level 40, try Frostfire Bolt in place of Frostbolt on enemies that live long enough for its cast. The
  Fire points after 40 are there to help it.
- Proposed: the first dungeons on the way are [Ragefire Chasm](/database/instances/389-ragefire-chasm),
  [the Deadmines](/database/instances/36-deadmines) and [Wailing Caverns](/database/instances/43-wailing-caverns), at
  levels 13, 16 and 17 in the game files. Their pages show what is known about each.

## What we do not know yet

The talents, their ranks and their texts come from the game files and have not been confirmed in the game; the
calculator labels them found in game files, unconfirmed. Where Ice Barrier's gate counts its points is not settled in
the files either, and the calculator reads it as points spent in Frost.

[Blizzard's deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) says abilities and talents may keep
changing through the beta, and the game files have changed since it was posted. If a later build changes a talent
this plan uses, this page says so.

The second talent set opens at level 40 in the calculator, the level the deep dive gives for Dual Specialization. This
guide plans one set. How the plan plays is not recorded here: its order is a plan, not a record of play.
