## Who it is for

A Mage from level 10, when the first talent point arrives, to level 60. The [talent calculator](/tools/talents/mage)
reads one point a level from the game files, 51 in all, and this plan spends every one of them.
[Blizzard's Mage deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) also starts talents at level 10.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: players who level mostly on their own, by questing, and want a plan that keeps enemies slowed and at a
  distance.

## Why this plan

[Blizzard's deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) adds Frostfire Bolt, which Mages learn
at level 40. It counts as Fire and as Frost, and Blizzard says the point is to give a Mage who mixes Fire and Frost
talents one spell that gains from both. In the game files, Fire talents also reach Frost spells: Incineration raises
the critical strike chance of Ice Lance among other spells, and Master of Elements returns mana on Fire and Frost
critical strikes. Accelerant, named Improved Fireball before build 70707, shortened the cast of Frostfire Bolt as well
as Fireball. Build 70707 changed its text, and we have not confirmed that it still names Frostfire Bolt. The
calculator shows each talent's own text.

The same deep dive cut the duration of Cone of Cold, so that farmers and boosters cannot kite as many enemies, and
weakened the slow from Improved Blizzard, so that Frost Mages cannot farm instances with area spells as easily.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: levels 10 to 40 in Frost, starting with the talents that speed up Frostbolt (Improved Frostbolt) and give
  chill effects a chance to freeze (Frostbite), then Ice Lance, Shatter and Fingers of Frost, which all work with
  frozen targets.
- Proposed: Ice Block and Cold Snap on the way, so that the plan reaches Ice Barrier at level 40.
- Proposed: levels 41 to 58 in Fire: Accelerant, Incineration, Ignite and Master of Elements for Frostfire Bolt and
  Ice Lance. Improved Fire Blast takes two points between them, which brings Fire to the 15 points Master of Elements
  needs. Since build 70707 Improved Fire Blast is in the third row, behind 10 points in Fire. The last two points go
  to Improved Frost Nova.
- Proposed: 3 of Incineration's 5 ranks. It had 3 ranks before build 70707.
- Proposed: no points in Improved Blizzard or Improved Cone of Cold, and leveling on one or two enemies at a time
  rather than on large pulls.

## How to play it

The plan lists every point in the order it is spent, one point a level, and opens in the calculator as this page
stores it.

### Editor to confirm

- Proposed: lead with Frostbolt. When Frostbite freezes the target, follow with Ice Lance: it deals more damage to
  frozen targets, and Shatter raises the critical strike chance of every spell against them.
- Proposed: when Fingers of Frost triggers, spend it on Ice Lance, since it lets the next spells treat the target as
  frozen.
- Proposed: keep Ice Block and Cold Snap for a fight that goes wrong. Cold Snap finishes the cooldowns of your other
  Frost spells.
- Proposed: from level 40, try Frostfire Bolt in place of Frostbolt on enemies that live long enough for its cast. The
  Fire points after 40 are there to help it.
- Proposed: the first dungeons on the way are [Ragefire Chasm](/database/instances/389-ragefire-chasm),
  [the Deadmines](/database/instances/36-deadmines) and [Wailing Caverns](/database/instances/43-wailing-caverns), at
  levels 13, 16 and 17 in the game files. Their pages show what is known about each.

## What we do not know yet

The talents, their ranks and their texts come from the game files and have not been confirmed in the game; the
calculator labels them found in game files, unconfirmed. Where Ice Barrier's gate counts its points is not settled in
the files either, and the calculator reads it as points spent in Frost. Accelerant's text changed in build 70707, so
whether it still shortens Frostfire Bolt is not confirmed.

[Blizzard's deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) warns that the beta could still
change Mage spells and talents, and the game files have changed since it was posted. This plan was checked against
build 1.60.1.70707. If a later build changes a talent it uses, this page says so.

The second talent set opens at level 40 in the calculator, the level the deep dive gives for Dual Specialization. This
guide plans one set. How the plan plays is not recorded here: its order is a plan, not a record of play.
