**TL;DR:** Blizzard shared its WoW: Forever beta numbers: 8,862,354 characters created, 9,959,014 PvP kills and 1,248,125 duels. The Hall of Thanes was the deadliest dungeon, the Kobold Miner the deadliest creature, and Hillsbrad Foothills saw the most PvP.

## What Blizzard posted
Blizzard closed the beta with a "Beta by the Numbers" card on [its official X account](https://x.com/Warcraft/status/2116245690200342529):

- **Characters created:** 8,862,354
- **PvP kills:** 9,959,014
- **Skyborne characters:** 1,889,084
- **Duels fought:** 1,248,125
- **Deaths from falling:** 1,157,204
- **Forsaken Paladins:** 612,096
- **Deadliest dungeon:** The Hall of Thanes
- **Deadliest creature:** Kobold Miner
- **Zone with the most PvP:** [Hillsbrad Foothills](/database/zones/1424-hillsbrad-foothills)

![Bar chart of six WoW: Forever beta totals from Blizzard: 9,959,014 PvP kills, 8,862,354 characters created, 1,889,084 Skyborne characters, 1,248,125 duels fought, 1,157,204 deaths from falling and 612,096 Forsaken Paladins](/news/figures/wow-forever-beta-stats-8-million-characters/beta-totals-chart.webp "Blizzard's six beta totals side by side. They count different things: characters, kills, duels and deaths.")

Side by side, two things stand out. There were more PvP kills than characters created. And testers fought only slightly more duels than they had deaths from falling.

## What it means for players
- **Skyborne are popular.** About 21% of all beta characters were Skyborne. Expect plenty of them at launch.
- **Forsaken Paladins are real.** Roughly 7% of characters tried the new combo. See our [racial changes recap](/news/wow-forever-racial-changes-character-creation).
- **Bring a group to the Hall of Thanes.** It killed more beta players than any other dungeon. Blizzard's [dungeon overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever) puts it beneath Ironforge in [Dun Morogh](/database/zones/1426-dun-morogh), for levels 13–18. Alliance players reach it from their capital; Horde groups have to travel into Alliance territory.
- **Hillsbrad will be busy.** If you level on a PvP ruleset, plan for fights there.
- **Character count is not player count.** Blizzard didn't share unique accounts, and many testers made several characters.

## What Everloot shows
Everloot's [census](/census) starts at launch, 23:00 UTC on November 4, and counts nothing from the beta. Once players upload, it shows who plays what by race, class, level and faction, so you'll be able to see whether Skyborne and Forsaken Paladins stay this popular.

It counts differently from Blizzard's card, too. Each character counts once, at the latest level seen. A count shows only when it covers at least 10 characters reported by at least 3 installs from at least 3 addresses, and it is rounded to the nearest 5. It covers players who run the addon and the players they target, mouse over or group with, so it leans toward people who install addons and the zones they play in.
