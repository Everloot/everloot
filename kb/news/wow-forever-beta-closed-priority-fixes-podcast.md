**TL;DR:** The WoW: Forever beta is officially closed after five weeks. Blizzard says it will spend a few days on priority issues and the game service, open character creation on Tuesday, October 27, and keep the WoW: Forever Podcast going next week. Launch is still November 4.

## What Blizzard said
In its [thank-you post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/thank-you-for-testing-the-wow-forever-beta/2390465), Blizzard wrote:

> "We're now quickly moving into a period of a few short days where we will do some focused work on priority issues and the game service itself." (Blizzard)

## What it means for players
- No more beta access. Your next login is character creation on Oct 27 ([our checklist](/news/wow-forever-character-creation-opens-tomorrow-checklist)).
- Blizzard did not list which issues are "priority." Players on [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/383560) flagged layering and class tuning.
- New podcast episodes are coming next week. Recent ones covered [Molten Core's new mechanics](/news/wow-forever-molten-core-new-mechanics-raid-tease).
