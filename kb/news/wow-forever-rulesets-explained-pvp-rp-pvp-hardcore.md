**TL;DR:** WoW: Forever has no realm list. You pick a ruleset instead: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, RP-PvP or, after launch, Hardcore. Name reservations open Tuesday, October 27, and the ruleset you pick then is hard to change, so decide before you log in.

## What a ruleset is
Per [Blizzard's ruleset article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/updated-10-22-choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever), each ruleset works like one large player pool instead of a single realm. You group, run dungeons and raid only with players on your own ruleset. Battlegrounds can mix rulesets, except Hardcore.

## The options
- **Normal:** the baseline game. PvP happens when you opt in.
- **PvP:** contested zones are dangerous. You can only make characters for one faction here, so an Alliance start in [Elwynn Forest](/database/zones/1429-elwynn-forest) locks you out of a Horde alt in [Durotar](/database/zones/1411-durotar).
- **Roleplaying:** for players who want to stay in character.
- **RP-PvP:** added in Blizzard's October 22 update. It arrives at launch on November 4, and Blizzard plans to offer it during name reservations.
- **Hardcore:** comes sometime after launch. Death is permanent, but a dead Hardcore character can move to another ruleset.

## Update, Oct 26: RP-PvP confirmed, one question open
Blizzard's Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield [confirmed on the forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/the-roleplay-pvp-ruleset-is-coming-to-wow-forever/2389502) that Blizzard reversed course after player feedback:

> "We will add a Roleplay-PVP Ruleset for launch on November 4." (Blizzard)

Blizzard is also "working hard" to let you pick RP-PvP and reserve names there starting Oct 27. The top open question from players: will one faction per account apply across both PvP and RP-PvP, so RP-PvP doesn't become a home for off-faction alts? Blizzard has not answered yet. We'll update this post when it does.

## What this means before October 27
- You can't hop between rulesets. Playing on another one means a new character.
- Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide, except that progress does not flow back into Hardcore.
- Set your preferred online language in Options, under Languages and Social. It has a 24-hour cooldown.
- Pick your capital early: [Stormwind City](/database/zones/1453-stormwind-city), [Ironforge](/database/zones/1455-ironforge), [Orgrimmar](/database/zones/1454-orgrimmar) or [Undercity](/database/zones/1458-undercity).
