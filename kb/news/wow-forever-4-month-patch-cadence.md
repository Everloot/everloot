**TL;DR:** WoW: Forever is targeting a major patch about every 4 months, roughly three a year, Principal Game Designer Kris Zierhut told [IGN](https://in.ign.com/world-of-warcraft-forever/275475/the-big-world-of-warcraft-forever-interview-blizzard-reveals-its-plan-for-the-next-great-mmo). PvP seasons will also run 4 months, and some content will unlock between major patches.

## What Blizzard said
Per [Wowhead's recap of the IGN interview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/wow-forever-is-aiming-for-a-4-month-patch-cadence-383640), the team tested pacing across Classic. Season of Discovery's 8–10-week patches felt too fast. Zierhut:

> "You might have patches with major releases four months apart, but there may be things that unlock two or three months in or one month into that."

## What it means for players
- **About 3 major patches a year,** each sized for smaller, local stories.
- **PvP seasons of 4 months** that line up with patches.
- **Staggered unlocks.** A patch won't drop everything on day one.
- **The road ahead.** Molten Core, Blackwing Lair and AQ are coming on a stretched timeline. Blizzard also mentioned the Emerald Dream, an explorable Grim Batol and pre-Cataclysm Gilneas as places it wants to go, per the [full interview summary](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/patch-cadence-future-locations-respeccing-ign-interview-on-wow-forever-383635).
- **Respecs.** Dual spec is in at launch, and a third spec is "not off the table." No on-the-fly talent swapping.
- **No player housing planned** right now.
