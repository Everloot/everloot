**TL;DR:** The WoW: Forever beta ended with a free-for-all: world bosses in the starting zones, legendaries like {item:19019} and {item:17182} off those bosses, Tier 1 sets from low-level mobs, and a Scourge invasion. None of it carries over. Live servers open November 4.

## What happened
According to [Wowhead's recap](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/world-bosses-legendaries-and-a-scourge-invasion-the-wow-forever-beta-is-over-383554), Blizzard used the final hours of the beta for a chaos event. A vendor outside the capital cities sold fireworks, a group speed scroll and a potion that boosted experience by 200%. World bosses, including Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, Thunderaan and an oversized Hogger, spawned in starting zones such as {zone:1429}, {zone:1411} and {zone:1420}.

Kills dropped legendaries, including {item:19019} and {item:17182}. Regular mobs in every starter zone dropped full Tier 1 sets and Molten Core weapons. To finish it off, Blizzard brought back the plague crates from the 2008 Scourge invasion event.

## What this means for WoW: Forever players
- **It was a stress party, not a preview of loot.** Legendaries from starter zones won't happen on live. On live, Thunderfury and Sulfuras are back to being long-term raid goals.
- **Name reservations open October 27** (1:00 p.m. PDT in North America), per [Blizzard's WoW Weekly](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24313106/dive-into-wow-forever-the-gods-of-zulaman-hallows-end-and-more-in-wow-weekly). Have your names ready.
- **Plan your first week now.** Use the [talent calculator](/tools/talents) and [professions planner](/tools/professions) before November 4.
