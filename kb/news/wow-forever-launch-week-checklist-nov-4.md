**TL;DR:** Blizzard published its global launch guide for WoW: Forever, which launches November 4. Before then: install it through Battle.net, make sure you can log in, agree on a ruleset and faction with friends, and have backup names ready. Your first login asks for an Experience Preset, and Blizzard says to start with Enhanced if unsure.

## What Blizzard said
The [launch guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24315643/return-to-azeroth-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) covers 1–60 across Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, more than 1,000 new quests and nine new five-player dungeons for levels 13–60. Wowhead's [summary](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/everything-you-need-to-know-for-wow-forevers-global-launch-383656) lists launch at 3:00 PM Pacific on November 4. That time comes from Wowhead; Blizzard's guide doesn't state it.

## Launch-week checklist
- **Install now.** Battle.net app → WoW: Forever → pick it under Game Version (and the right account if you have several) → Install.
- **Test your login.** If you're locked out, use Blizzard's "Can't Log In?" recovery before launch day, then check your region, license and security settings.
- **Update your PC.** Blizzard asks you to update your OS and graphics drivers and check the system requirements.
- **Know your access.** An active subscription or Game Time includes Forever. Skyborne requires the Heroic Pack or higher.
- **Pick a preset.** Classic (SD models, no quest blobs, one-bag and transmog off) or Enhanced (HD models, those features on). You can change it later in Options.
- **Coordinate your ruleset.** Normal, PvP, Roleplay or Roleplay-PvP. Blizzard says to match ruleset *and* faction with your friends and guild. Hardcore comes later.
- **Have backup names.** Full two-part names are first come, first served per region.
