**TL;DR:** Blizzard published its global launch guide for WoW: Forever, which launches November 4 at 3:00 p.m. PST. Before then: install it through Battle.net, make sure you can log in, agree on a ruleset and faction with friends, and have backup names ready. Your first login asks for an Experience Preset, and Blizzard says to start with Enhanced if unsure.

## What Blizzard said
The [launch guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24315643/return-to-azeroth-in-world-of-warcraft-forever) covers 1–60 across Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, more than 1,000 new quests and nine new five-player dungeons for levels 13–60. Blizzard's [launch announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-goes-live-globally-november-4) gives the time: 3:00 p.m. PST on November 4, which is 23:00 UTC. In some regions that is already November 5:

- **Americas (PST):** 3:00 p.m., November 4
- **UK (GMT):** 11:00 p.m., November 4
- **Europe (CET):** 12:00 a.m., November 5
- **Korea (KST):** 8:00 a.m., November 5
- **ANZ (AEDT):** 10:00 a.m., November 5

Raids don't open in launch week. Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair unlock on December 9, per Blizzard's [raid overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303009/world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overviewlocations-raid-sizes-and-attunement). Blizzard says that gives players time after launch to reach level 60 and prepare their groups.

## Launch-week checklist
- **Install now.** Battle.net app → WoW: Forever → pick it under Game Version (and the right account if you have several) → Install.
- **Test your login.** If you're locked out, use Blizzard's "Can't Log In?" recovery before launch day, then check your region, license and security settings. If you think the account was hacked, Blizzard says to secure your computer and email, request a restoration, change your password and add a Battle.net Authenticator.
- **Update your PC.** Blizzard asks you to update your OS and graphics drivers and check the system requirements.
- **Know your access.** An active subscription or Game Time includes Forever. Skyborne requires the Heroic Pack or higher.
- **Pick a preset.** Classic (SD models, no quest blobs, one-bag and transmog off) or Enhanced (HD models, those features on). You choose it before your ruleset, and you can change it later in Options. HD or SD models are also a per-character choice you can change at a Barbershop.
- **Coordinate your ruleset.** Normal, PvP, Roleplay or Roleplay-PvP. Blizzard says to match ruleset *and* faction with your friends and guild. Hardcore comes later.
- **Have backup names.** Full two-part names are first come, first served per region. You can hide the second name above your character in the Nameplates options, but it still shows in chat.
