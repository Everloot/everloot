**TL;DR:** From launch day, Everloot shows WoW: Forever as it is live. Item, spell and zone names come from the game's own files from the first hour. Drops, vendor lists, prices and where things are found depend on what players upload, so those pages start thin and fill in during the first days.

## What is there from the first hour

The [database](/database) reads the live game files. An item page such as {item:2589} names the item and shows what the files hold about it. The [class pages](/classes) and the talent planner use the live build's talents. The [changes timeline](/changes) lists the build the site is reading. Pages for Beta now say that Beta is no longer running.

## What fills in as people play

Some details aren't in the game files: which enemy drops an item, what a vendor sells, where a rare spawns, what an auction costs. Everloot only shows those after three different installs report the same thing. Until then, a page says it has no data rather than guessing. The [methodology page](/methodology) explains how reports become facts.

Market prices work the same way. A day's price for an item on a realm becomes public once three different reporters have scanned it.

## Helping it fill faster

Install the [addon](/addon), play, and upload after you log out, either through the [upload page](/upload) or with the [Desktop App](/desktop), which uploads for you after every logout or `/reload`. If you turn on answers in the Desktop App and leave it running, you can also type `/ask` in game and read the answer after your next `/reload`.

If a page looks wrong, check where it says the fact came from, then tell us what you saw in game.
