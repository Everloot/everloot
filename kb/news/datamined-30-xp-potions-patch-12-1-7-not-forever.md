**TL;DR:** WoW: Forever players, this one is not for you yet. **Unconfirmed:** [Wowhead found](https://www.wowhead.com/news/new-30-bonus-experience-potions-datamined-on-patch-12-1-7-ptr-383566) four new 30% bonus experience potions in the game files of the retail Patch 12.1.7 test server. Blizzard has not said how to get them.

> **UNCONFIRMED DATAMINE:** These potions were found in game files on a test server. They may change, ship differently or never ship.

## What was found
- **Four potions**, each named after one of the Talebound stories.
- **+30% experience** for **2 hours**.
- **Warband-bound**, so they can move between characters on one account.
- **How to get them:** unknown. Blizzard has not announced a source.

## Does this affect WoW: Forever?
No sign of it. Patch 12.1.7 and Warbands are retail systems. Nothing in the datamine points to WoW: Forever, which launches November 4 with its own rules. We will post if that changes.

*Datamine recap. Everloot has no addon data for these items.*
