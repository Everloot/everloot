**TL;DR:** Molten Core will not be the raid you solved in 2004. On the WoW: Forever Podcast, Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield said the old raid gets new mechanics, and Lead Encounter Designer Michael Nuthals teased three new bosses from the 10- and 20-player raids.

## Molten Core, revamped
Per [Wowhead's podcast recap](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/383543), Greenfield said:

> "You're not just going to stand there and tank and spank Golemagg, for example; there may be a little more to it." (Josh Greenfield, Blizzard)

The goal is to avoid a jarring drop-off between the new raids and an old one players already know by heart. Specific mechanics have not been shown.

## The raid philosophy
Nuthals described raids friends can clear together ("beer and pretzels") that still need coordination, by voice or with pings ([source](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/383546)). He teased:
- A **Barrow Deeps** boss where you manage a damage buff that has a cost.
- A **pair of spiders** he is "super excited for."
- An **undead crocolisk in Hyjal Summit** whose possessing spirit pops out, so the fight swaps between two phases.
- A future "home or manor" instance, likely tied to Manor Mistmantle in [Duskwood](/database/zones/1431-duskwood).

## What it means for players
- Old boss guides for Molten Core may not apply. Expect to relearn fights.
- Per Wowhead commenters citing the roadmap, Barrow Deeps and Hyjal arrive later, not at launch. Check the [Wowhead roadmap](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/content-release-roadmap).
