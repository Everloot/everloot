**TL;DR:** Molten Core will not be the raid you solved in 2004. On the WoW: Forever Podcast, Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield said the old raid gets new mechanics, and Lead Encounter Designer Michael Nuthals teased three new bosses from the 10- and 20-player raids. Those new raids open December 9; Blizzard hasn't dated Molten Core.

## Molten Core, revamped
Per [Wowhead's podcast recap](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/383543), Greenfield said:

> "You're not just going to stand there and tank and spank Golemagg, for example; there may be a little more to it." (Josh Greenfield, Blizzard)

He added that Blizzard used the words "revamped" or "reimagined" for it at BlizzCon. The goal is to avoid a jarring drop-off between the new raids and an old one players already know by heart. Specific mechanics have not been shown. Blizzard's [launch post](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-goes-live-globally-november-4) also sets the game's story before Molten Core.

## The raid philosophy
Nuthals described raids friends can clear together that still need coordination, by voice or with pings ([source](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/383546)):

> "we want folks to feel comfortable bringing their friends, we talk about beer and pretzels a lot." (Michael Nuthals, Blizzard)

He said each boss goes to Blizzard's QA team for playtesting, and the team iterates on mechanics testers find boring without piling on complexity. He teased:
- A **Barrow Deeps** boss where you manage a damage buff that has a cost.
- A **pair of spiders** he is "super excited for."
- An **undead crocolisk in Hyjal Summit** whose possessing spirit pops out, so the fight swaps between two phases.
- A future "home or manor" instance, likely tied to Manor Mistmantle in [Duskwood](/database/zones/1431-duskwood).

## What opens December 9
Blizzard's [raid overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303009/world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overviewlocations-raid-sizes-and-attunement) names three level 60 raids for December 9, all planned to reset weekly:
- **Barrow Deeps:** 10 players, eight bosses, inside Mount Hyjal with entrances through Mount Hyjal, [Winterspring](/database/zones/1452-winterspring) and Blackmaw Hold.
- **Hyjal Summit:** 20 players, 13 bosses, at the summit near Nordrassil. It opens with an ancient guardian, then lets you pick paths across the mountain.
- **Onyxia's Lair:** 40 players, in [Dustwallow Marsh](/database/zones/1445-dustwallow-marsh). Everyone needs the {item:16309} in their bags, from a faction attunement chain through Blackrock Spire. Blizzard says to build fire resistance first.

Molten Core is not on that list, and none of the posts we cite gives it a date or a raid size.

## What it means for players
- Old boss guides for Molten Core may not apply. Expect to relearn fights.
- If you want Onyxia on December 9, start the Drakefire Amulet chain early. Blizzard says to bring friends for the elite quests and leave time for several dungeon runs.
