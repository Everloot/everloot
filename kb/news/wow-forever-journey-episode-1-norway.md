**TL;DR:** Blizzard has started a "WoW: Forever Journey" video miniseries. Episode 1 is set in Norway and features Guzu, Snupy and Stian Blipp. **Unconfirmed:** Icy Veins reports the episode ties to the story of Mats Steen, but that claim comes from a Facebook post, and Blizzard has not said so.

## What we know
[Episode 1 is on Blizzard's YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yi4pwfhVW7g). It follows three well-known Norwegian personalities, Guzu, Snupy and Stian Blipp, as part of the run-up to the WoW: Forever launch on November 4. The series looks at why people still care about this game, more than twenty years on.

## What is not confirmed
> **UNCONFIRMED:** A link between this episode and the story of Mats Steen has been reported by Icy Veins, citing a Facebook post. Blizzard has not confirmed it. We will update this post if Blizzard does.

Mats Steen's story, told in the documentary *Ibelin*, is about friendships he built inside World of Warcraft. If the tie-in is real, it would be a meaningful nod. Until there is a source from Blizzard, treat it as a report, not a fact.

## Why it matters for Forever players
The Journey series is Blizzard telling the community story of WoW: Forever, not patch news. It is a good reminder that the people you meet in your first dungeon are a big part of why this version exists.
