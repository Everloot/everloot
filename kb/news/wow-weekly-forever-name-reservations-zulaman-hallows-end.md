**TL;DR:** WoW: Forever name reservations run from October 27 to November 3. The first level 60 raids open December 9. Hallow's End runs through November 2. In Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary, the Gods of Zul'Aman update is live.

## WoW: Forever news
Per [Blizzard's WoW Weekly for October 23](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24313106/dive-into-wow-forever-the-gods-of-zulaman-hallows-end-and-more-in-wow-weekly):
- **Name reservations:** October 27 to November 3. The window opens October 27 at 1:00 p.m. PDT in North America and 18:00 CET in Europe.
- **Rulesets replace realm selection.** You pick the kind of world you want to play in first, and each ruleset works like a large shared player pool.
- **Camping:** a Cooking Fire becomes a spot where players can rest, prepare and meet up.
- **Nine new five-player dungeons** for levels 13 to 60 across the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor.
- **Raids on December 9:** Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair. Onyxia requires the Drakefire Amulet attunement.
- **Podcast episode 5** is an end-of-beta special with members of the dev team.

## Elsewhere in WoW
- **Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary:** the Gods of Zul'Aman content update is live.
- **Hallow's End:** October 19 to November 2, with new decor and achievements in retail.

## What this means for WoW: Forever players
- Reserve names on day one. Popular names go fast.
- Start the Onyxia attunement early. [Head of Onyxia](/database/items/18422) is among the items to watch.
- Plan your builds in the [talent calculator](/tools/talents).
