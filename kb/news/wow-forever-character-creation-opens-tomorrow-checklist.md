**TL;DR:** WoW: Forever character creation opens tomorrow, Tuesday, October 27, at 1:00 PM PDT (18:00 CET in Europe). Eligible players can make up to 3 characters total across all rulesets until November 3. Install the client today so you are not downloading while the best names go.

## Start times
Per [Blizzard's announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303866/claim-your-world-of-warcraft-forever-character-names):
- **North America:** Oct 27, 1:00 PM PDT (also 1:00 PM in Arizona)
- **Europe:** Oct 27, 18:00 CET
- **Korea and Taiwan:** Oct 28, 07:00 local

> "Eligible players can create and reserve names for up to three characters total across all Rulesets." (Blizzard)

## Do this today
1. Update your graphics drivers.
2. Open the Battle.net app and let it update.
3. Pick World of Warcraft, then choose **World of Warcraft: Forever** under Game Version and select Install.
4. Decide your ruleset. The 3-character cap is shared: two PvP characters leave you one slot on Normal or Roleplaying. See our [rulesets explainer](/news/wow-forever-rulesets-explained-pvp-rp-pvp-hardcore).
5. On PvP rulesets you get one faction, so choose between an [Elwynn Forest](/database/zones/1429-elwynn-forest) or [Durotar](/database/zones/1411-durotar) start before you log in.
6. Have 2–3 backup names ready. Names are first come, first served.

## Why it matters
Blizzard says the [beta closed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/thank-you-for-testing-the-wow-forever-beta/2390465) so the team can fix priority issues before launch on November 4. Reservation week is your only chance to lock names before everyone else logs in, and capitals like [Stormwind City](/database/zones/1453-stormwind-city) and [Orgrimmar](/database/zones/1454-orgrimmar) will be crowded on day one.
