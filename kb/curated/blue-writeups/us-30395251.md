These are the development notes for the Beta build of October 8. The changes that matter most when leveling:

- Dungeons give more experience. Creature kills inside give about 20% more. A bug that shrank XP when a higher-level player was in the group is also fixed: each player's XP now depends on their own level, as in Classic Era. If someone in the group is far above the enemy's level, nobody gets XP, same as before. Blizzard expects the two changes together to raise dungeon XP a lot for most players.
- Warriors and Druids should get more Rage from being hit. Several bugs in that calculation are fixed, and Rage gains are now balanced around questing armor rather than tank armor.
- Creatures that dropped nothing can be clicked on again, as in Classic.
- Low ranks of the main damage and healing spells were retuned so that each new rank is always an upgrade. This covers Frostbolt, Fireball, Arcane Missiles, Smite, Lesser Heal and Heal, Lightning Bolt, Shadow Bolt and Wrath.
- The City of Dalaran dungeon is open for testing ([our notes on the new dungeons](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30550244-explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever)).
- The new Gelkis and Magram quests in [Desolace](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1443-desolace) now need level 35 (from 30). Their rewards were retuned to match.
- Every Beta player was given the Legacy Challenge (16 Legacy Points) to test the system, and Legacy respecs are cheap. Blizzard says the respec pricing applies only during the Beta.
- You can again swap several weapons within one global cooldown, for example with a macro.
- Voice chat can now use Discord, set by the party leader.

The post ends with three known issues, including Skyborne druid cat form textures that are still to come.
