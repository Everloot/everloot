The official World of Warcraft account put two pieces of news in one Bluesky post on October 20.

First, early name reservation for WoW: Forever opens on October 27 and lets you reserve names for up to three characters. The post links to Blizzard's character-name article. That article gives the start times (Europe 18:00 CET and North America 1:00 p.m. PDT on October 27; Korea and Taiwan 07:00 local time on October 28) and the November 3 end date. It also says the three characters count across all rulesets. [Our notes on that article](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30553826-claim-your-world-of-warcraft-forever-character-names) cover the details, including one point it leaves unclear: which purchases qualify.

Second, the Beta has been extended to 10 AM PDT on October 23. The post does not give the earlier end date. Kaivax's stress test post from the same day gives the same closing time and says the date is a Friday. The Beta also went offline for a few hours on October 21 for the public stress test ([times for each region](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30553354-heads-up-wow-forever-stress-test-on-october-21)).

The game launches on November 4 ([our overview](https://everloot.app/wow-forever)).
