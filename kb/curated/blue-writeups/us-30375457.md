Darkspear Islands is a new 15v15 battleground in WoW: Forever, set on islands off [Dustwallow Marsh](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1445-dustwallow-marsh) and open to levels 30 to 60. The Horde side is the Darkspear Raiders; the Alliance side is the Theramore Expeditionary Force.

How a match works, from the linked article:

- Four capture points. The more your team holds, the faster your score climbs.
- A flag in the middle of the map. Carry it to a base your team holds for extra points.
- The first team to 2000 points wins.

You can queue at a battlemaster in any capital city. You can also go to the faction bases in [the Barrens](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1413-the-barrens), where a portal takes you into the match: Horde at the Darkspear Raiders camp on the Merchant Coast, Alliance at the Theramore Expeditionary Force at Northwatch. Those bases also have the battleground quests and the reputation vendors.

Each side has its own reputation. Vendors sell trinkets, rings, cloaks, weapons, armor and consumables at different standings. Turn-in quests take Marks of Honor for reputation. At Exalted, a short questline gives the faction's tabard.

The article doesn't say whether Darkspear Islands joins the holiday-week battleground rotation described in the [PvP progression post](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30373680-how-pvp-progression-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-works).
