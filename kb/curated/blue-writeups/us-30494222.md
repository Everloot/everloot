These are the development notes for the Beta build that went up on October 15, Pacific time. The list is long, so here are the parts most players will notice.

- Racial abilities changed for every race. Three weapon-skill racials are replaced: Human Sword Specialization becomes Ruthless (2% critical strike chance), Orc Axe Specialization becomes Savage (1%), and Dwarf Mace Specialization becomes Earth Fury (2% critical damage and healing). Troll Regeneration bonuses go to 20% (from 10%). Tauren, Skyborne, Undead, Gnome and Night Elf racials changed too.
- Wands, and the Hunter's Auto Shot, now need you to stand still for 0.5 seconds before they fire, as in early WoW.
- More buffs now reach the whole raid: Battle Shout, Trueshot Aura, Moonkin Aura, Leader of the Pack, and the Grace of Air, Strength of Earth, Windfury and Flametongue totems. Grace of Air no longer conflicts with Windfury or Flametongue. Our [raid buff planner](https://everloot.app/tools/raid) counts these buffs as raid-wide. It reads them from the game files, so treat it as unconfirmed until they're seen in play.
- Paladins swap Blessing of Salvation for Salvation and Mass Salvation, which no longer take up a blessing.
- Mages get a new Frost Blast spell at level 24 and a redesigned Arcane Blast. Elemental Shamans get Elemental Mastery back as the 31-point talent. Shadow Priests get a new talent, Dark Inspiration.
- Horde players are now attacked on sight in Dalaran's open world. The City of Dalaran dungeon can still be entered.
- New players get a tutorial. The Auction House shows an equip-level column, and /who now matches subzones.
- Battleground Marks of Honor stack to 30 (from 20).

The post was edited on October 16. It doesn't mark what changed, so check the original for the full list.
