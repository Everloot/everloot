Early name reservation for WoW: Forever runs from October 27 to November 3. In that window you can create characters to hold their names, up to three in total. The limit counts every ruleset together: two characters on a PvP ruleset leave room for one on Normal or Role-Play. Blizzard says the three-character cap applies only during the reservation period. Blizzard has since said a Roleplay-PvP ruleset is coming at launch, and it is working to have it ready for name reservation ([our notes](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30576546-the-roleplay-pvp-ruleset-is-coming-to-wow-forever)).

Start times, as given in the linked news article:

- Europe: October 27, 18:00 CET
- North America: October 27, 1:00 p.m. PDT
- Korea: October 28, 07:00 KST
- Taiwan: October 28, 07:00 CST

Names go first come, first served, and no name is guaranteed. To take part, install World of Warcraft: Forever from the Game Version menu in the Battle.net app, pick a ruleset and create your characters.

One thing is unclear. The article's main text says pre-purchasing the Skyborne Heroic Pack, the Skyborne Epic Pack or the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection gives access. Its footnote says the Digital Upgrade or the Collector's Edition is required, and that the benefit applies only to the account it was bought for. The article does not say whether these are the same products under different names. If you are not sure, check what your account owns before October 27.

Characters made during the October 21 stress test were wiped and do not carry over. The game itself launches on November 4 ([our overview](https://everloot.app/wow-forever)).
