The first three raids of WoW: Forever open on December 9, not on launch day. All three are for level 60 characters. From the linked news article:

- The Barrow Deeps: 10 players, eight bosses. It lies under Mount Hyjal, with entrances from Mount Hyjal, [Winterspring](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1452-winterspring) and the Blackmaw Hold dungeon.
- Hyjal Summit: 20 players, 13 bosses, at the top of Mount Hyjal near Nordrassil. After the first fight the raid splits into several routes you can choose between.
- Onyxia's Lair: 40 players, in [Dustwallow Marsh](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1445-dustwallow-marsh). One three-phase fight with heavy fire damage, so Blizzard suggests building fire resistance first.

Every player entering Onyxia's Lair needs the Drakefire Amulet in their bags (it does not have to be equipped). It comes from a long faction quest chain:

- Alliance starts with "Dragonkin Menace" from Helendis Riverhorn in the [Burning Steppes](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1428-burning-steppes), then works through Blackrock Depths and "The Great Masquerade" in Stormwind.
- Horde starts with "Blood and Thunder" from Warlord Goretooth in Kargath, [Badlands](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1418-badlands), then goes through Lower Blackrock Spire, Thrall, Rexxar in [Desolace](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1443-desolace) and the drake Emberstrife in Dustwallow Marsh.

Both chains finish in Upper Blackrock Spire against General Drakkisath and include elite quests, so bring friends and start early. The game launches on November 4 ([our overview](https://everloot.app/wow-forever)), which leaves five weeks to level and attune before the raids open. Blizzard says all raids are currently planned to reset weekly.

To work out buffs for a 10, 20 or 40 player group, try the [raid buff planner](https://everloot.app/tools/raid).
