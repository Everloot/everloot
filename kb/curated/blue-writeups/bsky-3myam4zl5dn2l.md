This short Bluesky post links to a video on the official World of Warcraft YouTube channel, "Your Leveling Journey Past 30". The post itself says only that levels 30 to 60 in WoW: Forever take you into parts of Azeroth that were previously unknown.

The video's description names two of those areas: the Riverglades and Mount Hyjal. Other Blizzard posts this month say more about both:

- The Riverglades holds Krol'dok Stronghold, a dungeon for levels 40–45 ([our dungeon notes](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30550244-explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever)).
- Mount Hyjal holds two of the three raids that open on December 9, the Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit ([our raid notes](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30552119-world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overview-locations-raid-sizes-and-attunement)).

These notes use the post and the video's description, not a full review of the video. We can't yet tell you which other new areas the video shows or what levels each one is for. Neither the Riverglades nor Mount Hyjal has a zone page on Everloot yet. The zones that do are in the [zone index](https://everloot.app/database/zones) and on [the map](https://everloot.app/map).
