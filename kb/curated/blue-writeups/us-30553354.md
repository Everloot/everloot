Kaivax posted the full timetable the day before the October 21 stress test. The Beta went offline and WoW: Forever opened on the hardware each region will use at launch.

Start and end times, as posted:

- CEST: October 21, 19:00 to 03:00
- BST/IST: October 21, 18:00 to 02:00
- EDT: October 21, 5:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.
- CDT: October 21, 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- PDT: October 21, 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- NZDT: October 22, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- AEDT: October 22, 8:00 a.m. to noon
- Taipei: October 22, 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m.
- Seoul: October 22, 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.

Converted, every window ended at the same moment. The European ones started four hours before the rest, so Europe got eight hours and everyone else four. The post does not explain the difference.

To join, players updated their graphics drivers, opened the Battle.net app and selected World of Warcraft. In the Game Version menu they chose "World of Warcraft: Forever Stress Test" under In Development and installed it, then pressed Play once the test started and picked a test realm. Any WoW account could take part, and no subscription was needed.

Stress test characters and names were deleted at the end, and the Beta reopened afterwards. The Beta then closes for good at 10:00 a.m. PDT on Friday, October 23. Name reservation starts on October 27 ([our notes](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30553826-claim-your-world-of-warcraft-forever-character-names)).
