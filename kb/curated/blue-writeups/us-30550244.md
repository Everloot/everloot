Blizzard has named the nine new five-player dungeons coming with WoW: Forever. Six are in the Eastern Kingdoms and three in Kalimdor, and together they cover levels 13 to 60. The forum post is a short teaser; the list below comes from the news article it links.

- Hall of Thanes, levels 13–18: under Ironforge, in [Dun Morogh](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1426-dun-morogh)
- Ruins of Lordaeron, 15–20: the ruined city on top of the Undercity, [Tirisfal Glades](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1420-tirisfal-glades)
- Excavation Site, 24–29: above Whelgar's Excavation in the [Wetlands](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1437-wetlands)
- City of Dalaran, 28–33: [Alterac Mountains](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1416-alterac-mountains)
- The Drowned City, 35–40: off the [Stranglethorn Vale](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1434-stranglethorn-vale) coast, west of Grom'gol, with an underwater entrance
- Krol'dok Stronghold, 40–45: the Riverglades
- Alcaz Prison, 48–53: Alcaz Island, [Dustwallow Marsh](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1445-dustwallow-marsh)
- Blackmaw Hold, 55–60: [Azshara](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1447-azshara)
- Shaper's Terrace, 58–60: the northern hills of [Un'Goro Crater](https://everloot.app/database/zones/1449-un-goro-crater)

A few things to plan around. Hall of Thanes is inside Alliance land and Ruins of Lordaeron inside Horde land, so groups from the other faction have to travel there. City of Dalaran needs an attunement chain first: Alliance starts with "An Alarming Request" from Emissary Jacques in Southshore, Horde with "Key to the City" and "Prison Break-In" from Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill. Shaper's Terrace uses power crystals you collect around Un'Goro.

The Riverglades has no zone page on Everloot yet.
