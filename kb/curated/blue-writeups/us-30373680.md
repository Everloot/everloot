Blizzard's article on PvP in WoW: Forever describes two things you earn, Honor and Rank Points.

- Honor is a currency. You get it from killing enemy players, finishing battlegrounds and doing PvP quests, with extra in the battleground featured for its holiday week. Blizzard says Honor gains are now more consistent across battlegrounds, so players shouldn't feel they have to pick Alterac Valley just for Honor.
- Rank Points move you up the original ranks, toward High Warlord for the Horde or Grand Marshal for the Alliance. They come from battlegrounds and PvP quests, with the same holiday-week boost. There is a weekly cap.

Honor buys two PvP gear sets, a rare (blue) one and an epic one. An epic piece is the blue piece plus an Emboldened Seal, and you can only buy seals once you reach the right rank. New and reworked items fill gaps for specs that were missing options, for example healing, melee and caster sets for Shamans. The article lists rank rewards from 1 to 14: the PvP trinket comes at rank 2, mounts at rank 11 and epic weapons at rank 14.

Each PvP season includes a journey of weekly tasks that award Honor and Rank Points. Three tasks a season also give a Legacy Point, and they can still be finished in later seasons. Your rank, Rank Points and the rewards you can buy are on the Player vs. Player tab of the character pane.

The article doesn't give the size of the weekly cap, Honor prices or season dates. For the new 15v15 battleground, see [our notes on Darkspear Islands](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30375457-battle-for-the-darkspear-islands-in-world-of-warcraft-forever).
