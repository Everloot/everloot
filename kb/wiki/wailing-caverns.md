## Overview

The caves under the Barrens take their name from the moaning of the wind that runs through them. Naralex, a night
elf druid, came down here to link the caves' water to the Emerald Dream and bring the dry plains above back to life.

It went wrong. The Emerald Nightmare took hold of him in his sleep, and he could not wake. Through him the Nightmare
spread into the caves: the animals turned deviate, and most of his followers lost their minds.

## Getting there

The entrance lies in {zone:1413}, southwest of the Crossroads. [Find it on the map](/map?zone=1413&x=47.7&y=35). The
Crossroads flight point is the nearest in the game files.

## History

His followers had been a proud circle of druids who shared his goal for the Barrens. Twisted along with him, they
became the Druids of the Fang. They spread the corruption through the caves and the Barrens, and guard his body
against anyone who would wake him. One of his last loyal disciples, Muyoh, is still trying to bring him back.
